# Phishing Attack

## Description
Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers impersonate a trusted organization or person to trick users into revealing sensitive information such as passwords, banking details, or personal data.

## Common Indicators
- Fake login pages
- Suspicious email links
- Urgent messages requesting action
- Unknown email sender
- Requests for confidential information

## Severity
High

## MITRE ATT&CK
T1566 - Phishing

## Prevention
- Verify sender email addresses
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Do not click suspicious links
- Use email filtering solutions
- Educate users about phishing

## Example
An employee receives an email claiming to be from Microsoft asking them to reset their password. The link redirects to a fake login page.
