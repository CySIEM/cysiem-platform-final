## Weekly Plan

### Week 1

- Team 1: Collect and normalize logs
- Team 2: Build asset intelligence
- Team 3: Configure Ollama and AI models
- Team 4: Design correlation engine
- Team 5: Prepare RAG pipeline
- Team 6: Design dashboard

### Week 2

Teams begin exchanging outputs and integrating modules.

### Week 3

System integration and testing.

### Week 4

Bug fixing, optimization, and final demo.

## Goal

Build a complete AI-powered SIEM platform where each module communicates smoothly with the next.
